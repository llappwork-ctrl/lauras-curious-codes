# QA Case Studies 🔎

These investigations come from The Bug Detective Agency, my hands-on coding and QA learning project.

Some investigations are intentional challenges created by an AI collaborator for me to explore. Others are real defects encountered while developing the game. Each case study identifies which kind it is and separates my contribution from AI assistance.

## Investigations

| Case study | Type | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| [Evidence Board Repair](evidence-board-repair.md) | Real development defect | Verified cause, committed correction, and reported functional retest |

## How I describe evidence

- **Verified** — Available code, history, or recorded observations support the specific claim.
- **Partially documented** — Some evidence exists, but details such as reproduction steps, attribution, or retest results are missing.
- **Needs recovery** — The available record is insufficient to establish what happened.

A status applies to the evidence described. It does not imply that every possible regression test passed.

## Contributions and testing

Each case study explains what I personally observed, investigated, changed, or tested, and what an AI collaborator diagnosed, implemented, or checked. Missing evidence is stated explicitly.
